Delivered as part of the Cybersecurity + AI Meetup organized by Build Club and Hewlett Packard Enterprise in Pune.

The session looked at practical ways AI is already changing security work:

- Detecting and countering deepfakes.
- Preventing sensitive-data leaks.
- Improving threat detection and prioritization.
- Automating parts of incident response.

Rather than treating AI security as a future concept, the talk focused on tools and use cases that security teams can explore today.
